How the stylist thinks

Every saree–belt pair is scored on light–dark contrast, hue harmony (analogous vs complementary), echo of the saree's zari/border tone, fabric weight vs belt finish, and occasion formality. A RandomForest learns those trade-offs from 10,752 labelled pairings; the final ranking blends the model's score (65%) with the colour-theory rubric (35%).
